\begin{abstract}
We address the problem of determining which variables of a large dynamical
system are driven by the system and which evolve autonomously. Existing
causal-detection methods cannot address this question at scale: we measured
full pairwise convergent cross mapping at $\approx$31{,}700 compute-hours
for a single scan of $10^4$ variables, $k$-nearest-neighbour conditioning
became sample-starved by 18 conditioning dimensions, and PCMCI with partial
correlation produced mediated false positives near synchrony. We first
isolate three mechanisms by which \emph{learned} readouts fail on such
systems: maturity collapse of difference-based importance under Takens
redundancy, typicality substitution in masked-reconstruction scores, and the
inability of ensembling to remove shared bias. Each is demonstrated with a
separating experimental signature. Together they imply a corrected
statistic, an amortised Granger/transfer-entropy-type quantity we call
\emph{Masked-Autoencoder Conditional Excess} (MACE): the gain in one-step
predictability of a variable when a learned low-dimensional code of the
entire remaining system is added to a flexible model of the variable's own
history. The code comes from a small ensemble of unsupervised masked
autoencoders and each per-variable readout is a ridge regression, so a
complete scan of 71{,}721 variables runs in minutes on a single consumer
laptop, four orders of magnitude below the pairwise equivalent. Every
analysis embeds a \emph{ghost channel}, a circularly shifted copy of a real
variable whose score must be zero, giving each dataset a built-in
falsification test that requires no ground truth. On synthetic systems with
known membership MACE attains top-10 precision of 30/30 across three system
seeds. Across four open real-world domains it behaves as theory predicts and
fails informatively where it does not: in \emph{C.~elegans} it ranks the
canonical motor-command circuit as the brain's driven core without access to
anatomical labels and tracks a genetic silencing of the command hub AVA
neuron by neuron; in a 71{,}721-neuron zebrafish recording the driven core is
spatially coherent and posterior-midline; in clinical electroencephalography,
where the statistic maps seizure spread rather than onset, seizures
concentrate network drive while per-event maps fail to replicate; and in 77
years of daily sea-level pressure the driven core is the deep tropics, while
an embedded solar-forcing channel scores zero, confirming the intended
insensitivity to exogenous sources. We state semantics and limits precisely,
and release code, pre-registrations, and a ledger including all negative
results.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Causal structure discovery from time series is well served by
established tools at small scale. Convergent cross mapping exploits Takens' embedding theorem to detect
coupling between pairs of variables on shared attractors
\citep{sugihara2012,takens1981,ye2015}; constraint-based frameworks such as PCMCI (the Peter--Clark, or PC,
algorithm combined with a Momentary Conditional Independence test)
condition away spurious associations across moderate variable
counts \citep{runge2019,runge2019nc}; and Granger causality and transfer entropy define the
underlying conditional-predictability quantity
\citep{granger1969,schreiber2000}. The question increasingly posed in practice, however, is which of ten
thousand observed variables the system drives. That question lies outside
the demonstrated operating range of every existing tool. Neural implementations of Granger causality report tens to $\sim$100
variables \citep{tank2021}, and amortised causal discovery more broadly
remains demonstrated at comparable scale \citep{lowe2022}; our own wall-clock measurement of pairwise CCM under the full protocol
($\sim$1.1\,s per pair at $n{=}2{,}000$) projects a single $10^4$-variable scan at roughly 31{,}700
compute-hours ($\approx$3.6 years); and the natural accelerations fail in our tests (cross-mapping against
principal-component summaries scores its own control at the maximum;
$k$NN conditioning \citep{kraskov2004} collapses at 18 conditioning
dimensions in a two-seed synthetic test).

No existing tool closes this. Our own measurements above leave pairwise CCM
wall-clock infeasible at this width, and its natural acceleration via
$k$NN conditioning \citep{kraskov2004} sample-starved by 18 dimensions;
PCMCI \citep{runge2019} is untested at this width and, on the synthetic
chains examined later in this paper (Section~\ref{sec:map}), acquires
mediated false positives near synchrony rather than degrading gracefully;
and the closest learned relatives, neural Granger causality
\citep{tank2021} and amortised causal discovery more broadly
\citep{lowe2022}, stop at tens to $\sim$100 variables and ship no
embedded, ground-truth-free validity check of their own. \textbf{No
existing method estimates variable-level drivenness at $V \gtrsim 10^3$
with a built-in, ground-truth-free validity check; this is the gap this
paper closes.}

This paper closes that gap. What is new is not the causal quantity:
Granger causality and transfer entropy \citep{granger1969,schreiber2000,barnett2009}
already define what excess-over-self predictability measures, masked
autoencoders \citep{he2022mae,devlin2019} are an existing
representation-learning tool rather than one introduced here, and the
ghost channel is a direct descendant of surrogate-data testing
\citep{theiler1992}. The novelty is in what makes that existing quantity
usable at $V \gtrsim 10^3$, in three parts. \textbf{(i)} We isolate, with
separating experimental signatures rather than an aggregate accuracy
drop, three distinct mechanisms by which learned causal readouts fail on
redundant dynamical systems: maturity collapse of difference-based
importance, typicality substitution in masked reconstruction, and
ensembling's inability to remove shared bias (Section~\ref{sec:mechanisms}).
\textbf{(ii)} These mechanisms jointly dictate, rather than merely
motivate, a single corrected statistic: an amortised
Granger/transfer-entropy-type quantity whose self-baseline cancels
typicality by construction and whose embedded ghost channel supplies the
built-in, ground-truth-free validity check that closes the gap above.
The statistic is amortised, via unsupervised compression, to the tens of
thousands of variables demonstrated later in this paper, where the classical and
neural incumbents above are wall-clock infeasible, sample-starved,
mediation-prone, or undemonstrated (Section~\ref{sec:method}); we
validate it where membership is known by construction
(Section~\ref{sec:synthetic}). \textbf{(iii)} Beyond synthetic
benchmarks, we deploy the statistic across four open real-world domains
at a cost small enough to place the method within reach of any
laboratory with a workstation, and subject it to a stronger evidence
class than correlational validation alone: an interventional test in which the statistic,
computed purely observationally, tracks a targeted genetic silencing of
the \emph{C.~elegans} command hub AVA neuron-by-neuron in directions
stated before the data was opened, alongside a measured
method-selection map (Sections~\ref{sec:real}--\ref{sec:map}).
The rest of this paper is structured as follows.
Section~\ref{sec:mechanisms} isolates the three failure mechanisms.
Section~\ref{sec:method} derives the proposed method from them and states
its cost. Section~\ref{sec:synthetic} validates it on systems whose driven
set is known by construction, and Section~\ref{sec:real} deploys it across
four open real-world domains, including an interventional test.
Section~\ref{sec:map} distils an empirical method-selection map,
Section~\ref{sec:limits} states the limitations, and
Section~\ref{sec:concl} concludes.

\subsection*{Terminology across fields}
This work spans dynamical systems, machine learning, neuroscience,
clinical electrophysiology and climate science; we define the recurring
terms once, here. \emph{Driven / drivenness}: a variable is driven if the
state of the rest of the system carries information about its next value
beyond the variable's own history; its degree is the excess statistic of
Eq.~\ref{eq:excess}. \emph{Autonomous}: evolving under its own dynamics
only; the opposite of driven. \emph{Source (exogenous root)}: a variable
that influences others but is driven by nothing observed; invisible to the
statistic by design. \emph{Delay embedding}: the vector of a variable's
recent lagged values, which for deterministic systems reconstructs the
underlying state \citep{takens1981}. \emph{Ghost channel}: a circularly
time-shifted copy of a real variable, embedded in the analysis as a
negative control. \emph{Masked autoencoder}: a neural network trained to
restore randomly hidden inputs from the remainder, yielding a compressed
\emph{code} of the whole system. \emph{Ensemble / consensus}: several
identically trained networks differing only in random initialisation, and
the average of their readouts. \emph{Self-baseline}: the predictability of
a variable from its own history alone, subtracted in Eq.~\ref{eq:excess}.
\emph{Top-$k$ precision}: the fraction of the $k$ highest-ranked variables
that are true positives. \emph{Average precision (AP)}: the area under the
precision-recall curve; its chance level equals the positive-class
fraction (the \emph{baseline}). \emph{AUROC}: area under the
receiver-operating-characteristic curve; 0.5 is chance.
\emph{Ictal / interictal}: during / between epileptic seizures.
\emph{Connectome}: the anatomical wiring diagram of a nervous system.
\emph{Reanalysis}: a historical gridded reconstruction of the atmosphere
from observations. \emph{Teleconnection}: a statistical linkage between
climate variability in distant regions. \emph{Pre-registration}: fixing
predictions and analysis choices in a written artifact before inspecting
the data.

Registration discipline varied by study and we state it exactly: the EEG
and climate deployments were registered in a protocol document before their
data was accessed; the wild-type worm predictions were fixed in the analysis
script's header before execution, with two animals reserved untouched for
held-out confirmation; the intervention directions were stated in the
working ledger before the silenced-worm data was opened; the zebrafish
analysis was exploratory apart from its in-code ghost gate. Negatives are
reported at the same volume as positives, and every number below is
reproducible from the released code and public data.

\section{Three failure mechanisms}
\label{sec:mechanisms}

The gap above is not closed by pointing a larger learned model at the problem, and it is worth being precise about why. The obvious learned readouts do not degrade gracefully as $V$ grows; they fail for three specific and separable reasons, and each reason turns out to prescribe part of the remedy. We isolate them here, before proposing anything, so that the statistic of Section~\ref{sec:method} can be read as the construction these failures leave standing rather than as one design choice among many.

All experiments in this section use coupled logistic-map networks (with
heterogeneous and stochastic variants) where ground truth is known by
construction; full protocols are in the released ledger. Every analysis
embeds a \emph{ghost channel}, our standing negative control, in the
tradition of surrogate-data hypothesis testing
\citep{theiler1992,ebisuzaki1997}. The ghost
is built by taking one real variable's series and rotating it in time by a
large random offset: the rotated copy retains every single-channel property
of real data---the same distribution of values, the same autocorrelation,
the same spectrum---but its moments no longer line up with anyone else's,
so it is, by construction, causally connected to nothing. A valid analysis
must therefore score it at zero; any nonzero ghost score measures the
analysis's own artifact level. This is the logic of surrogate-data testing,
embedded as a channel and scored by the same machinery as every real
variable.

\begin{mechanism}[Maturity collapse of difference-based importance]
\label{mech:maturity}
Difference-based scores (leave-one-covariate-out and relatives) measure a
trained model's loss when one input is removed. When a cause's imprint is
redundant across several observed effects (generic for dynamical
systems, since any single effect's delay embedding reconstructs its driver
\citep{takens1981}), the difference collapses to zero \emph{precisely when
training succeeds}: a mature model re-routes around any single removed
input. The separating signature, which distinguishes this collapse from
overfitting, is the following. On a hub-structured network, checkpointing
each model across its own training trajectory shows redundant-parent
scores rising, peaking at partial maturity, and collapsing toward zero by
epoch 50 in the seed mean (complete in one seed, partial in the other).
Unique-parent scores grow monotonically throughout in both seeds
($0.02\!\to\!0.28$). The crossing of these two curves is the synthetic
signature. On real neural data
(\emph{C.~elegans}, dense connectome redundancy \citep{white1986,cook2019}) the collapse endpoint
replicates: mature leave-one-out gains are exactly zero---AUROC $0.500$ to
three decimals in all three worms tested, and no intermediate checkpoint
yields informative scores. This is the
function-class dependence of importance gaps \citep{xu2020} expressed along
the training trajectory: such scores work \emph{because of} restricted
capacity, not despite it. Figure~\ref{fig:maturity} shows the crossing.
\end{mechanism}

\begin{mechanism}[Typicality substitution in masked reconstruction]
\label{mech:typicality}
With difference-based scores ruled out by redundancy, the natural move is to stop removing inputs and instead ask how well the rest of the system reconstructs a channel outright.

Scoring a channel by how well a trained masked autoencoder
\citep{he2022mae} reconstructs it from the rest is a natural foundation-model-style probe of coupling \citep{devlin2019,he2022mae}. At scale, on homogeneous pools, it inverts: on a 1{,}000-channel
benchmark (a coupled minority of $\sim$100 among $\sim$900 autonomous
channels), reconstruction ranks periodic autonomous channels highest (0.76),
the ghost at 0.74, chaotic autonomous channels at 0.29, and true members
last (0.25). The decoder's loss-optimal completion of a masked slot is the
\emph{family-typical} trajectory, so reconstruction measures typicality, not coupling. Coupled channels,
perturbed off the family manifold by their drive, are the least typical. Bottleneck width ($8\!\to\!256$) and masked-only losses fail to
repair the inversion, because neither changes the optimal blind completion
(fill-value ablations, run at $V{=}30$ where no inversion exists, showed no
effect there). Figure~\ref{fig:typicality} shows
the inversion.
\end{mechanism}

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.55\linewidth]{figs/fig_typicality.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{Masked reconstruction measures typicality, not

\begin{mechanism}[Ensembles average variance, not bias]
\label{mech:ensemble}
A natural remedy for a learned readout's failures is ensembling
\citep{lakshminarayanan2017}: training many models from independent
initialisations and using their consensus. We tested this directly: eight masked autoencoders on one fixed
system, only the seed varying. The consensus average-precision curve
rises with ensemble size ($0.111\!\to\!0.148$) with the expected
$1/\sqrt{M}$ deceleration, exactly as the law of large numbers predicts,
and converges to a biased limit: the ghost's reconstruction score is
$0.740\pm0.004$ \emph{across the eight independent minima}. The typicality
artifact is a property of data-plus-objective, reproduced in every basin;
averaging over minima cannot remove it. Figure~\ref{fig:lln} shows both
curves.
\end{mechanism}

\section{Proposed method: MACE}
\label{sec:method}

The three mechanisms do not merely motivate the correction below; each rules out a class of readout, and what survives all three rulings is a single construction. Mechanism~\ref{mech:maturity} rules out any score defined as a difference across redundant alternatives, forcing the comparison to be made against a variable's \emph{own} history rather than against its peers. Mechanism~\ref{mech:typicality} rules out reporting a reconstruction quality absolutely, forcing that own-history term to be \emph{subtracted} rather than merely reported, so that family-typicality cancels instead of ranking. Mechanism~\ref{mech:ensemble} rules out the consensus itself as evidence of validity, forcing an artifact meter that is external to the models and re-measured on every dataset---the ghost channel---to be scored by the same machinery as every real variable. A self-referenced, subtractive, externally controlled readout is what remains; the rest of this section specifies it. Let $x_q(t)$ be variable $q$
after per-variable standardisation and first-differencing (stationarity is a prerequisite; on drifting data the circular-shift
ghost inflates and the scan must be discarded rather than thresholded, and the ghost must
be checked, since it does not announce failure). Form delay embeddings ($E=3$
throughout) and the joint state $Z(t)$.

We call the complete procedure \textbf{MACE}, for
\emph{Masked-Autoencoder Conditional Excess}: a masked autoencoder
supplies
the compressed code, and the statistic it computes is the conditional excess
of Eq.~\ref{eq:excess}, the gain in predictability conditional on that code.
The ensemble and the embedded ghost control are components of MACE rather
than separate procedures.

\paragraph{Code.} Train $M$ masked autoencoders (independent
initialisations) on $Z$: random channel subsets are corrupted each batch,
the loss is computed on masked positions only \citep{he2022mae}, and a hard
linear bottleneck of width $b \ll \dim Z$ yields codes $c_m(t)$.

\paragraph{Readout.} For each variable $q$ and model $m$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{excess}_m(q) \;=\;
R^2\!\left[\,x_q(t{+}1)\;\middle|\;\phi\big(\text{own lags of } q\big)\oplus
c_m(t)\right]
\;-\;
R^2\!\left[\,x_q(t{+}1)\;\middle|\;\phi\big(\text{own lags of }
q\big)\right],
\label{eq:excess}
\end{equation}
with both terms ridge regressions fit on a training segment and evaluated
on a held-out contiguous tail ($R^2$ clamped at zero), and $\phi$ a
polynomial feature map (degree 2 on the quadratic-map synthetics, where it
is exact; degree 3 on all real data; both degrees shown on the
heterogeneous pool). The reported score is the consensus mean
over models. The subtraction cancels typicality by construction (a channel
predictable from its own structure gains nothing from the code); the
statistic is not a difference across redundant alternatives, so
Mechanism~\ref{mech:maturity} does not apply (the architecture-identical
absolute readout was empirically stable across 5--100 encoder-training
epochs at $V{=}30$; the excess readout reuses those saved encoders); and
its lineage is explicit: a Granger/transfer-entropy-type quantity
\citep{granger1969,schreiber2000,barnett2009} --- the two are equivalent
for Gaussian variables \citep{barnett2009}
amortised to large $V$ by unsupervised compression
\citep{janzing2010}---CCM's
convergence-with-library-size criterion makes the analogous self-control
implicitly \citep{sugihara2012}; Eq.~\ref{eq:excess} makes it explicit and
scalable.

\paragraph{Semantics.} Positive excess means the system's state carries
information about $X$'s next step beyond $X$'s own past: $X$ is
\emph{driven}. We call this property \emph{drivenness}, a variable's rank
under the statistic its drivenness percentile (0--1 scale), and the
top-$k$ variables by consensus excess a system's \emph{driven core}. A pure source is perfectly predicted by its own history and
is therefore invisible. This is a designed, testable asymmetry:
known-exogenous
variables must score zero (verified below on solar forcing; a genetic
silencing approximates it, since the silenced hub falls sharply but not
to zero, retaining residual input).

\paragraph{The self-model's function class is critical.} With a linear
self-baseline the correction fails (a linear fit of a quadratic map leaves
residual self-structure that the code then ``explains'', scoring periodic
channels $+0.26$---which is Mechanism~\ref{mech:typicality} returning under a weakened self-model: an under-powered own-history fit leaves exactly the family-typical structure that the code can then re-supply, and the typicality ordering reappears inside the excess itself, periodic channels first); with polynomial features matched to the dynamics
(degree 2 suffices on the quadratic maps), self-baselines match theory to
three decimals and the confound is cancelled rather than suppressed. On
real data, where no exact function class exists, the ghost and known-root
channels are the practical check.

\paragraph{Cost, and why it is the practical argument.} Every result in
this paper was produced on one consumer laptop with a single RTX 3070
GPU (8\,GB) and 28\,GB of system memory. No cluster, no multi-GPU node,
and no cloud budget was used at any point. Table~\ref{tab:cost} gives the
measured wall-clock costs.

Three properties produce this. First, the encoder is trained \emph{once}
per system and is unsupervised, so its cost is independent of how many
variables one subsequently queries. Second, each per-variable readout is a
ridge regression on a handful of features, at $\sim$8\,ms per variable,
so scoring scales linearly in $V$ rather than quadratically. Third, saved
encoder weights make any new readout (a different self-baseline, a new
statistic, a re-analysis under a reviewer's suggestion) an evaluation
rather than a retrain, at seconds per variable-sweep.

\paragraph{Control.} The ghost of Section~\ref{sec:mechanisms} is a component of the statistic, not an auxiliary experiment: one real variable is duplicated, circularly shifted by a large random offset, and passed through the encoder and the readout alongside every other variable, so each scan reports its own artifact floor without reference to ground truth. Ensembling cannot serve this role. Mechanism~\ref{mech:ensemble} showed that a data-plus-objective bias is reproduced in every independent minimum, so agreement across models, seeds or bottleneck widths measures only variance; the consensus mean above is therefore used for what it can do---variance reduction, and a cross-model spread diagnostic---while validity is delegated to the ghost. A scan whose ghost does not sit at zero is discarded, not thresholded.

The consequence is worth stating plainly, because it determines who can
use this. A pairwise scan of the zebrafish recording would require
roughly ninety GPU-years; the same question, asked through MACE,
took fourteen minutes on a laptop that also ran the authors' text editor.
The method is not merely faster than the alternative at scale, it is the
difference between an analysis that a small laboratory can run on
existing hardware and one that no laboratory would attempt.

\label{sec:synthetic}

which a minority sub-web is coupled (sparse random DAG) and the remaining
channels are autonomous; membership is known by construction. At $V{=}1001$ (93--95 members by seed; baseline precision
$\approx$0.094):

\section{Real-world validation and deployments}
\label{sec:real}

We deploy the statistic on four open real-world domains.

They are ordered by how much independent ground truth each affords, from most to least, and each therefore tests something its predecessor could not. The worm has named cells, a published connectome and a targeted genetic silencing; the zebrafish has no labels at all, only three-dimensional coordinates, so its only available check is geometric---at $500\times$ the width; clinical EEG has an event annotation but no channel-level truth, and asks whether the statistic tracks a change of regime within one subject; the atmosphere has no truth of any kind, only a forcing that is exogenous by physics and a century of established circulation structure to fall against. All datasets
are public and unrestricted. Registration status is stated per study
below; the released protocol document and analysis-script headers
are the citable artifacts. Table~\ref{tab:gates} summarises the validity
gates across all deployments.

\subsection{\emph{C.~elegans}: label-free recovery of the motor-command circuit, and an interventional test}

We start where the statistic can be checked hardest: the only system in this paper that offers named cells, an independently mapped wiring diagram, and a perturbation that can be aimed at a specific hub.

\textbf{Wild type.} Whole-brain calcium imaging of five immobilised worms
(109--135 neurons, $\sim$3{,}100 samples) \citep{kato2015}.
Predictions fixed in the analysis script before execution: ghost
$\approx 0$; sensory neurons (hand-curated canonical classes; $n{=}8/6/1$
per development worm, so per-worm evidence is weak though the direction is
uniform) less driven than command/motor neurons in the constant
environment; positive correlation with anatomical in-weight
\citep{white1986,cook2019}. All three passed on the three animals used
during analysis development (hereafter \emph{development worms}) and
again on two animals reserved since the study's declaration and opened only
after predictions were fixed, with the strongest anatomy correlations of
the five ($+0.30$, $+0.34$). The top-10 most-driven neurons are 10/10 command/motor class in two of
the three development worms, with the command core topping the two held-out
rankings as well: AVA, AVE, RIM, AIB, RIB, RIV, RME, SMDV and
ventral-cord motor neurons. This is the canonical motor-command ensemble
of the immobilised worm \citep{kato2015}, obtained without access to
anatomical or functional annotations.

\textbf{Intervention.} The same pipeline on five worms with the
reverse-command hub AVA silenced via a histamine-gated channel
\citep{kato2015}. Directions stated in the working ledger before the
silenced-worm data was opened: the silenced hub shows the largest decrease within the command core (drivenness percentile $0.92\!\to\!0.38$); its
module and motor pool collapse (AVE $-0.28$, RIB $-0.29$, VB01 $-0.64$,
RMED $-0.49$, VA01 $-0.45$); ghost scores remain at zero in all five animals. Two further observations emerge from the pooled per-cell contrast, and both deserve unpacking rather than a bare number.

\textbf{RIM and AIB retain drive almost unchanged} ($-0.08$, $-0.08$,
class-pooled across the five-animal cohorts, though not uniformly in every
single cell, since one silenced animal's RIMR sits low while its RIML
tops the ranking) while every other module member collapses. This is mechanistically
notable rather than incidental. Sordillo and Bargmann established that RIM
is not a passive relay of upstream command input but carries its own
bistable depolarised/hyperpolarised dynamic that shapes reversal behaviour
on its own terms \citep{sordillo2021}, and Piggott et al.\ document a
strong AIB$\to$RIM synaptic route capable of operating independently of
AVA's own command synapses \citep{piggott2011}. A network-drivenness score
for this pair that survives near-intact when the circuit's dominant hub is
silenced is the pattern that anatomy predicts \emph{if} RIM's intrinsic
dynamics and the AIB$\to$RIM route are supplying the residual drive. The identical
pattern would also arise from a readout that is simply
insensitive to whatever we manipulate, and five animals under one
perturbation cannot distinguish the two. Confirming the first reading over
the second would need independent replication, a manipulation that targets
AIB or RIM directly rather than routing through AVA, and single-cell (not class-pooled) consistency, which the RIML/RIMR split
above already shows is not yet in hand.

\textbf{Drive reorganises onto the forward hub AVB} ($+0.34$; AVB is
recorded in three of the five silenced animals and the rise is carried by
two of them, so this observation is the weaker of the pair; ALA rises by a
comparable margin, $+0.30$, Table~\ref{tab:intervention}, but, unlike RIM, AIB and AVB, has no established place in the AVA/AVB/AIB/RIM
reversal-command architecture in the sources we cite, so we report it in
the table and leave it uninterpreted rather than assign it a mechanism we
cannot support). AVB is the forward-command counterpart to AVA within the
same architecture, driving forward locomotion as AVA drives reversal, and
the same whole-brain recordings that motivated the wild-type analysis
established that this worm's global dynamics organise into a small number
of alternating command states rather than one fixed hierarchy
\citep{kato2015}. A rise in AVB's own drivenness once the reversal hub is
silenced is the pattern that organisation predicts if the rest of the
network reorganises around whichever command pathway remains rather than
simply losing an input channel. With the signal carried by only two of
three recorded animals, however, a small-sample artifact is at least as
economical an explanation, which is why we call this the weaker of the two
observations.

We report both as candidate observations pending adjudication against the
reversal-circuit literature \citep{gray2005,sordillo2021}; independent of
whether these observations are novel, an observational statistic tracked a
targeted genetic intervention neuron-by-neuron in the stated directions.
Table~\ref{tab:intervention} and Figure~\ref{fig:worm} give the per-cell
contrast.

\subsection{Zebrafish: a whole-vertebrate-brain map}

The worm tests the statistic against labels. The zebrafish removes them and asks whether the method survives a five-hundred-fold increase in width, where the only check left is whether the driven core is anywhere in particular.

ZAPBench provides 71{,}721 segmented neurons $\times$ 7{,}879 timesteps of
light-sheet imaging in a larval zebrafish \citep{zapbench}. On a
2{,}241-step window (open-loop, rotation, dark), the full-brain excess map is computed in minutes on a laptop GPU. The ghost scores $-0.031$; $\sim$2{,}800 neurons clear the $+0.01$
excess threshold; and the driven core is spatially
coherent: the top-1{,}000 neurons are $1.78\times$ more tightly packed than
random draws (median nearest-neighbour distance 16.8 vs.\ $29.8\pm0.6$),
concentrated posteriorly and against the midline, consistent with the
hindbrain/midline territory where internally generated motor dynamics are reported to reside
\citep{dunn2016}---in a window dominated by the dark condition. Region
names await atlas registration and are not claimed
(Figure~\ref{fig:fish}).

\subsection{Clinical EEG: a confirmed effect and an instructive null}

Both animal deployments had a stable organisation to recover. Clinical recordings do not, and they are where the statistic's semantics can be violated by the clinical question itself: the quantity clinicians want is the onset zone, which is a source, and sources are invisible here by construction. What remains testable is whether drive reorganises between conditions---and, as it turns out, where that reorganisation stops being resolvable at the level of a single event.

CHB-MIT scalp EEG, subject chb01: 23 channels, 256\,Hz; six of the seven
annotated seizures are analysed (the seventh record was not in our
retrieved subset) \citep{shoeb2009,physionet}, in a field where seizures are increasingly
understood as network-organisation disorders
\citep{kramer2012,friston2011,vicente2011}. One caveat governs all outputs:
seizure-onset zones are \emph{sources}, and sources are invisible to
this statistic by design. The statistic maps the spread network, not the
origin.
Results: max $|$ghost$|$ 0.034 across the 14 windows (no numeric
tolerance was pre-specified; ghost magnitudes across the other deployments
range from $\sim$0.000 on the synthetics to $0.041$ on one wild-type
worm); drivenness \emph{concentration} (top-4 channel share) is
higher during seizures than between them (0.68 vs.\ 0.47; five of six
within-record pairs, with the exception, record 03, in the opposite
direction). Seizures therefore recruit the network into a driven regime
(Figure~\ref{fig:eeg}).

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.5\linewidth]{figs/fig_eeg.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{Seizures concentrate network drive.} Top-4 channel share
of total excess, interictal versus ictal, per record with an annotated
seizure. Five of six records move in the predicted direction; the exception (record 03, red) is included.}
\label{fig:eeg}
\end{figure} The third pre-registered prediction failed, and this failure is itself
informative: per-event driven
patterns do not replicate across the same patient's seizures (mean pairwise
Spearman 0.08 over 15 pairs). Whatever the cause---genuine per-event
variability or single-window noise---the practical warning stands: single-event connectivity maps at these
window lengths should not be treated as patient signatures without
per-patient replication; we tested one subject.

\subsection{Climate: a tropical driven core and an invisible solar forcing}

The three biological deployments share one inference target: a single organism's internal organisation. The atmosphere supplies what none of them can---a driver that is exogenous by physics rather than by construction, which can be dropped in among ten thousand real variables and must score nothing.

Seventy-seven years of daily sea-level pressure (NCEP/NCAR Reanalysis-1;
10{,}512 cells, $\sim$28{,}000 days) \citep{kalnay1996}, deseasonalised and
differenced, with the SILSO daily sunspot number \citep{silso} embedded as
an additional channel. Pre-registered gates: ghost $-0.005$;
\textbf{sunspot channel $-0.003$}---a real exogenous forcing, embedded among ten thousand atmospheric variables, scores nothing: the
cleanest
field test of the statistic's designed insensitivity to exogenous
sources; and the driven core is spatially
clustered (top-500 median nearest-neighbour distance 0.0435 vs.\
$0.0676\pm0.0048$ under a spatial null). The exploratory map, fixed to
carry no prior claim, reproduces well-established large-scale structure: 99\% of the 500
most-driven cells lie within $30^{\circ}$ of the equator (mean $|$lat$|$
$6.1^{\circ}$ vs.\ $45.6^{\circ}$ for the grid). This is the Walker/ENSO
belt \citep{wallace1981,bjerknes1969},
where tropical pressure is subordinate to planetary-scale
circulation \citep{bjerknes1969}, while polar and storm-track cells,
dominated by locally generated baroclinic dynamics \citep{chang2002},
rank most autonomous. Every cell shows positive excess; a single connected fluid contains no
autonomous members (Figure~\ref{fig:climate}).

\paragraph{What the four establish together.} No one of these deployments is decisive, and they are not four repetitions of the same demonstration. Two of them converge: two nervous systems, imaged by different techniques at a five-hundred-fold difference in width and analysed without labels in either case, place the driven core on the same class of machinery---the canonical motor-command ensemble in the worm, the posterior-midline territory associated with internally generated motor dynamics in the fish. Three of them test the same designed blindness, by unrelated natural means: a hub silenced genetically (AVA, percentile $0.92\!\to\!0.38$---an approximate source, and it falls accordingly), a forcing that is exogenous by physics (the sunspot channel, $-0.003$ among $10{,}512$ atmospheric variables), and a structure that is a source by clinical definition (the seizure-onset zone, which the statistic correctly declines to find); the synthetic AR(1) family is the same test under construction. Blindness to sources is what makes the statistic falsifiable rather than merely descriptive, and it is the property most likely to be wrong; it survived three independent natural attempts to break it. Every deployment cleared its ghost gate without recourse to ground truth (Table~\ref{tab:gates}), across $V$ from $23$ to $71{,}721$ and $n$ from $2{,}000$ to $\sim$28{,}000. The EEG null marks the boundary this makes visible: the statistic resolves a change in network organisation between conditions well before it can be trusted to map a single event.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

The statistic's semantics are narrow by construction, and the evidence above has boundaries that no ghost channel can detect. We state both, roughly in the order in which they would bite a new user.

(1) Drivenness only; sources are invisible, and a source-detection
complement is future work. (2) Top-$k$ semantics; weakly driven members are
indistinguishable from non-members. (3) Validity is established down to $n\approx2{,}000$ samples; no
learned variant we tested succeeded on a 48-point circadian dataset, and
the intermediate regime is unmeasured. (4)
Stationarity is an essential assumption. (5) Single recordings per condition in the biological analyses; the
intervention contrast is five animals per cohort from one preparation; the
EEG analysis covers one subject; absolute excess magnitudes on the worm are small (top-band 0.02--0.23
across cohorts); synthetic validation is confined to map-type generator
families. (6) The self-model's function class governs transfer;
the ghost and known-root channels are the practical guard. (7) The
statistic estimates a known causal quantity; the contribution is scale,
controls, failure analysis and validation, not the concept.

\section{Conclusions and future work}
\label{sec:concl}

Three failure mechanisms explain why learned causal readouts break on
redundant dynamical systems. The correction they imply, excess over
self-predictability computed against a compressed code of everything else,
turns chance-level detection into high-precision membership
scans. It does so at widths where pairwise scanning is infeasible in our
wall-clock measurements and local conditioning is sample-starved, and it
behaves as theory predicts across worm, fish, clinical and geophysical
data, including under a genetic intervention.

That closes the gap stated at the outset in both of its halves: variable-level drivenness is estimable at $V \gtrsim 10^3$---demonstrated to $71{,}721$ variables in minutes on a laptop---and every scan carries a control that travels with the data rather than with a benchmark, so a result on a system for which nobody has ground truth can still be falsified. The evidence is not only correlational: on the worm, a statistic computed with no access to the perturbation tracked a targeted genetic silencing cell by cell, in directions written down before the data was opened. What the statistic cannot do is name sources, and the same self-referenced construction that makes it scalable and self-checking is what makes it blind to them---a source-detection complement built on the same subtraction is the natural next step, and the ghost channel is already the instrument for validating it. The operational protocol that found every failure here
(embedded ghosts, known-root channels, trajectory checkpointing,
pre-registration, precision-recall against stated baselines, published
negatives) is, we believe, as transferable as the statistic itself.

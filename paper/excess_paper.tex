\begin{abstract}
We address the problem of determining which variables of a large
dynamical system are driven by the system and which evolve autonomously.
Existing causal-detection methods cannot address this question at scale: we measured full pairwise convergent cross mapping (CCM) at
$\sim$1.1\,s per pair under the full protocol ($\approx$31{,}700
compute-hours for one scan of $10^4$ variables); $k$-nearest-neighbour
($k$NN) conditioning became sample-starved at 18 conditioning dimensions in
our two-seed synthetic tests; and PCMCI (the Peter--Clark algorithm with
momentary conditional independence) with partial correlation produced
mediated false positives near synchrony on synthetic chains. We first
isolate three mechanisms by which
\emph{learned} readouts fail on such systems---maturity collapse of
difference-based importance under Takens redundancy, typicality substitution
in masked-reconstruction scores, and the inability of ensembling to remove
shared bias---each demonstrated with a separating experimental signature.
These mechanisms jointly imply a corrected statistic---an amortised
Granger/transfer-entropy-type quantity we call
\emph{excess-over-self predictability}: the gain in one-step predictability
of a variable when a learned low-dimensional code of the entire remaining
system is added to a flexible model of the variable's own history. The code
comes from a small ensemble of unsupervised masked autoencoders; each
per-variable readout is a ridge regression, so a complete scan of 71{,}721
variables runs in minutes on a laptop. Every analysis embeds a \emph{ghost
channel}---a circularly shifted copy of a real variable---whose score must
be zero, giving each dataset a built-in falsification test that needs no
ground truth. On synthetic systems with known membership the statistic attains top-10
precision of 30/30 across three system seeds, with non-member excess never
exceeding $+0.0005$ across the $\sim$900 autonomous channels of each
system. Across four open real-world domains the statistic behaves as theory
predicts, and fails informatively where it does not. In
\emph{C.~elegans} it ranks the canonical motor-command circuit as the
brain's driven core without access to anatomical or functional labels
(top-10 all command/motor in two of three analysis-development worms, the
command core topping both held-out rankings; all predictions stated in
advance pass in five of five), and it tracks a genetic silencing of the
command hub AVA neuron-by-neuron. In a 71{,}721-neuron zebrafish recording
the driven core is spatially coherent ($1.78\times$ tighter than chance)
and posterior-midline. In clinical electroencephalography (EEG; the
statistic maps seizure spread, not onset), seizures measurably concentrate
network drive, while per-event spread maps fail to replicate---a negative
result with practical implications for single-event connectivity mapping.
In 77 years of daily sea-level pressure the driven core is the deep
tropics (99\% of top cells within $30^{\circ}$ of the equator), and an
embedded solar-forcing channel scores zero within the ghost tolerance
($-0.003$), confirming the statistic's intended insensitivity to exogenous
driving sources. We state semantics and limits
precisely---drivenness only, top-$k$ precision, validated down to
$n\approx2{,}000$ samples---and
release code, pre-registrations, and a ledger including all negative
results.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Causal structure discovery from time series is well served by
established tools at small scale. Convergent cross mapping exploits Takens' embedding theorem to detect
coupling between pairs of variables on shared attractors
\citep{sugihara2012,takens1981,ye2015}; constraint-based frameworks such as PCMCI---the Peter--Clark (PC)
causal-discovery step combined with a momentary-conditional-independence
(MCI) test---condition away spurious associations across moderate variable
counts \citep{runge2019,runge2019nc}; and Granger causality and transfer entropy define the
underlying conditional-predictability quantity
\citep{granger1969,schreiber2000}. The question increasingly posed in practice, however---which of ten
thousand observed variables the system drives---lies outside the
demonstrated operating range of every existing tool. Neural implementations of Granger causality report tens to $\sim$100
variables \citep{tank2021}, and amortised causal discovery more broadly
remains demonstrated at comparable scale \citep{lowe2022}; our own wall-clock measurement of pairwise CCM under the full protocol
($\sim$1.1\,s per pair at $n{=}2{,}000$) projects a single $10^4$-variable scan at roughly 31{,}700
compute-hours ($\approx$3.6 years); and the natural accelerations fail in our tests (cross-mapping against
principal-component summaries scores its own control at the maximum;
$k$NN conditioning \citep{kraskov2004} collapses at 18 conditioning
dimensions in a two-seed synthetic test).

This paper makes three contributions. \textbf{(i)} We isolate, with
separating experimental signatures, three mechanisms by which learned
causal readouts fail on redundant dynamical systems
(Section~\ref{sec:mechanisms}). \textbf{(ii)} We derive from them a corrected,
scalable statistic with a built-in falsification control
(Section~\ref{sec:method}), and validate it where truth is known
(Section~\ref{sec:synthetic}). \textbf{(iii)} We deploy it across four open
real-world domains---including an interventional test in which the
statistic tracks a targeted genetic silencing neuron-by-neuron---and
distil a measured method-selection map (Sections~\ref{sec:real}--\ref{sec:map}).
\subsection*{Terminology across fields}
This work spans dynamical systems, machine learning, neuroscience,
clinical electrophysiology and climate science; we define the recurring
terms once, here. \emph{Driven / drivenness}: a variable is driven if the
state of the rest of the system carries information about its next value
beyond the variable's own history; its degree is the excess statistic of
Eq.~\ref{eq:excess}. \emph{Autonomous}: evolving under its own dynamics
only; the opposite of driven. \emph{Source (exogenous root)}: a variable
that influences others but is driven by nothing observed; invisible to the
statistic by design. \emph{Delay embedding}: the vector of a variable's
recent lagged values, which for deterministic systems reconstructs the
underlying state \citep{takens1981}. \emph{Ghost channel}: a circularly
time-shifted copy of a real variable, embedded in the analysis as a
negative control. \emph{Masked autoencoder}: a neural network trained to
restore randomly hidden inputs from the remainder, yielding a compressed
\emph{code} of the whole system. \emph{Ensemble / consensus}: several
identically trained networks differing only in random initialisation, and
the average of their readouts. \emph{Self-baseline}: the predictability of
a variable from its own history alone, subtracted in Eq.~\ref{eq:excess}.
\emph{Top-$k$ precision}: the fraction of the $k$ highest-ranked variables
that are true positives. \emph{Average precision (AP)}: the area under the
precision-recall curve; its chance level equals the positive-class
fraction (the \emph{baseline}). \emph{AUROC}: area under the
receiver-operating-characteristic curve; 0.5 is chance.
\emph{Ictal / interictal}: during / between epileptic seizures.
\emph{Connectome}: the anatomical wiring diagram of a nervous system.
\emph{Reanalysis}: a historical gridded reconstruction of the atmosphere
from observations. \emph{Teleconnection}: a statistical linkage between
climate variability in distant regions. \emph{Pre-registration}: fixing
predictions and analysis choices in a written artifact before inspecting
the data.

Registration discipline varied by study and we state it exactly: the EEG
and climate deployments were registered in a protocol document before their
data was accessed; the wild-type worm predictions were fixed in the analysis
script's header before execution, with two animals reserved untouched for
held-out confirmation; the intervention directions were stated in the
working ledger before the silenced-worm data was opened; the zebrafish
analysis was exploratory apart from its in-code ghost gate. Negatives are
reported at the same volume as positives, and every number below is
reproducible from the released code and public data.

\section{Three failure mechanisms}
\label{sec:mechanisms}

All experiments in this section use coupled logistic-map networks (with
heterogeneous and stochastic variants) where ground truth is known by
construction; full protocols are in the released ledger. Every analysis
embeds a \emph{ghost channel}, our standing negative control, in the
tradition of surrogate-data hypothesis testing
\citep{theiler1992,ebisuzaki1997}. The ghost
is built by taking one real variable's series and rotating it in time by a
large random offset: the rotated copy retains every single-channel property
of real data---the same distribution of values, the same autocorrelation,
the same spectrum---but its moments no longer line up with anyone else's,
so it is, by construction, causally connected to nothing. A valid analysis
must therefore score it at zero; any nonzero ghost score measures the
analysis's own artifact level. This is the logic of surrogate-data testing,
embedded as a channel and scored by the same machinery as every real
variable.

\begin{mechanism}[Maturity collapse of difference-based importance]
\label{mech:maturity}
Difference-based scores (leave-one-covariate-out and relatives) measure a
trained model's loss when one input is removed. When a cause's imprint is
redundant across several observed effects---generic for dynamical systems,
since any single effect's delay embedding reconstructs its driver
\citep{takens1981}---the difference collapses to zero \emph{precisely when
training succeeds}: a mature model re-routes around any single removed
input. The separating signature, which distinguishes this collapse from
overfitting, is the following. On a hub-structured network, checkpointing
each model across its own training trajectory shows redundant-parent
scores rising, peaking at partial maturity, and collapsing toward zero by
epoch 50 in the seed mean (complete in one seed, partial in the other).
Unique-parent scores grow monotonically throughout in both seeds
($0.02\!\to\!0.28$). The crossing of these two curves is the synthetic
signature. On real neural data
(\emph{C.~elegans}, dense connectome redundancy \citep{white1986,cook2019}) the collapse endpoint
replicates: mature leave-one-out gains are exactly zero---AUROC $0.500$ to
three decimals in all three worms tested---and no intermediate checkpoint
yields informative scores. This is the
function-class dependence of importance gaps \citep{xu2020} expressed along
the training trajectory: such scores work \emph{because of} restricted
capacity, not despite it. Figure~\ref{fig:maturity} shows the crossing.
\end{mechanism}

\begin{figure}[t]

\begin{mechanism}[Typicality substitution in masked reconstruction]
\label{mech:typicality}
Scoring a channel by how well a trained masked autoencoder
\citep{he2022mae} reconstructs it from the rest is a natural foundation-model-style probe of coupling \citep{devlin2019,he2022mae}. At scale, on homogeneous pools, it inverts: on a 1{,}000-channel
benchmark (a coupled minority of $\sim$100 among $\sim$900 autonomous
channels), reconstruction ranks periodic autonomous channels highest (0.76),
the ghost at 0.74, chaotic autonomous channels at 0.29, and true members
last (0.25). The decoder's loss-optimal completion of a masked slot is the
\emph{family-typical} trajectory, so reconstruction measures typicality, not
coupling---and coupled channels, perturbed off the family manifold by their
drive, are the least typical. Bottleneck width ($8\!\to\!256$) and masked-only losses fail to
repair the inversion, because neither changes the optimal blind completion
(fill-value ablations, run at $V{=}30$ where no inversion exists, showed no
effect there). Figure~\ref{fig:typicality} shows
the inversion.
\end{mechanism}

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.55\linewidth]{figs/fig_typicality.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{Masked reconstruction measures typicality, not coupling.}
Consensus masked-reconstruction $R^2$ per channel class at $V{=}1001$
(8-encoder ensemble). Periodic autonomous channels and the ghost---which
carries no causal information by construction---score highest; genuinely
coupled members score lowest. The causal ordering is inverted.}
\label{fig:typicality}
\end{figure}

\begin{mechanism}[Ensembles average variance, not bias]
\label{mech:ensemble}
A natural remedy for a learned readout's failures is ensembling
\citep{lakshminarayanan2017}: training many models from independent
initialisations and using their consensus. We tested this directly: eight masked autoencoders on one fixed
system, only the seed varying. The consensus average-precision curve
rises with ensemble size ($0.111\!\to\!0.148$) with the expected
$1/\sqrt{M}$ deceleration, exactly as the law of large numbers predicts,
and converges to a biased limit: the ghost's reconstruction score is
$0.740\pm0.004$ \emph{across the eight independent minima}. The typicality
artifact is a property of data-plus-objective, reproduced in every basin;
averaging over minima cannot remove it. Figure~\ref{fig:lln} shows both
curves.
\end{mechanism}

\section{The excess-over-self statistic}
\label{sec:method}

The three mechanisms jointly motivate a single correction. Let $x_q(t)$ be variable $q$
after per-variable standardisation and first-differencing (stationarity is a prerequisite; on drifting data the circular-shift
ghost inflates and the scan must be discarded rather than thresholded---the
ghost must be checked, it does not announce failure). Form delay embeddings ($E=3$
throughout) and the joint state $Z(t)$.

\paragraph{Code.} Train $M$ masked autoencoders (independent
initialisations) on $Z$: random channel subsets are corrupted each batch,
the loss is computed on masked positions only \citep{he2022mae}, and a hard
linear bottleneck of width $b \ll \dim Z$ yields codes $c_m(t)$.

\paragraph{Readout.} For each variable $q$ and model $m$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{excess}_m(q) \;=\;
R^2\!\left[\,x_q(t{+}1)\;\middle|\;\phi\big(\text{own lags of } q\big)\oplus
c_m(t)\right]
\;-\;
R^2\!\left[\,x_q(t{+}1)\;\middle|\;\phi\big(\text{own lags of }
q\big)\right],
\label{eq:excess}
\end{equation}
with both terms ridge regressions fit on a training segment and evaluated
on a held-out contiguous tail ($R^2$ clamped at zero), and $\phi$ a
polynomial feature map (degree 2 on the quadratic-map synthetics, where it
is exact; degree 3 on all real data; both degrees shown on the
heterogeneous pool). The reported score is the consensus mean
over models. The subtraction cancels typicality by construction (a channel
predictable from its own structure gains nothing from the code); the
statistic is not a difference across redundant alternatives, so
Mechanism~\ref{mech:maturity} does not apply (the architecture-identical
absolute readout was empirically stable across 5--100 encoder-training
epochs at $V{=}30$; the excess readout reuses those saved encoders); and
its lineage is explicit: a Granger/transfer-entropy-type quantity
\citep{granger1969,schreiber2000,barnett2009} --- the two are equivalent
for Gaussian variables \citep{barnett2009}
amortised to large $V$ by unsupervised compression
\citep{janzing2010}---CCM's
convergence-with-library-size criterion makes the analogous self-control
implicitly \citep{sugihara2012}; Eq.~\ref{eq:excess} makes it explicit and
scalable.

\paragraph{Semantics.} Positive excess means the system's state carries
information about $X$'s next step beyond $X$'s own past: $X$ is
\emph{driven}. We call this property \emph{drivenness}, a variable's rank
under the statistic its drivenness percentile (0--1 scale), and the
top-$k$ variables by consensus excess a system's \emph{driven core}. A pure source is perfectly predicted by its own history and
is therefore invisible---a designed, testable asymmetry: known-exogenous
variables must score zero (verified below on solar forcing; a genetic
silencing approximates it---the silenced hub falls sharply but not to
zero, retaining residual input).

\paragraph{The self-model's function class is critical.} With a linear
self-baseline the correction fails (a linear fit of a quadratic map leaves
residual self-structure that the code then ``explains'', scoring periodic
channels $+0.26$); with polynomial features matched to the dynamics
(degree 2 suffices on the quadratic maps), self-baselines match theory to
three decimals and the confound is cancelled rather than suppressed. On
real data, where no exact function class exists, the ghost and known-root
channels are the practical check.

\paragraph{Cost.} Training is $\sim$57\,s per model at $V=71{,}721$
(and $\sim$70\,s at $V=10{,}512$; both from archived model timestamps) on
a consumer GPU with a streaming input pipeline; each readout is a ridge fit
($\sim$8\,ms per variable, $\sim$10 minutes for a full 71{,}721-variable
scan); saved encoders make any new readout an evaluation rather than a
retrain.

\label{sec:synthetic}

$\approx$0.094):

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.92\linewidth]{figs/fig_synthetic.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{The excess statistic at $V{=}1001$ (system seed 0).}
Channels ranked by consensus excess. The top of the ranking is pure
coupled members (blue); all 907 autonomous channels (orange) and the ghost
(star) pin at or below zero. The statistic is a top-$k$ detector: weakly

\label{fig:synthetic}

\textbf{top-$k$ detector} (members whose drive share falls below the
readout's noise floor receive slightly negative excess and rank below the
zero-pinned non-members, so full-population AUROC is uninformative even
when top-$k$ precision is perfect---0.28 here by mechanism, not accident;
average precision and top-$k$ against stated baselines are the correct

critical} (archived single-model AP 0.25 vs.\ consensus 0.24; its chief
value is the cross-model variance diagnostic). ``False alarm'' throughout means non-member excess above the ghost
floor; the largest non-member excess observed in any synthetic system is
$+0.0005$, an order of magnitude below the weakest top-20 member.

\subsection{\emph{C.~elegans}: label-free recovery of the motor-command circuit, and an interventional test}

\textbf{Wild type.} Whole-brain calcium imaging of five immobilised worms
(109--135 neurons, $\sim$3{,}100 samples) \citep{kato2015}.
Predictions fixed in the analysis script before execution: ghost
$\approx 0$; sensory neurons (hand-curated canonical classes; $n{=}8/6/1$
per development worm, so per-worm evidence is weak though the direction is
uniform) less driven than command/motor neurons in the constant
environment; positive correlation with anatomical in-weight
\citep{white1986,cook2019}. All three passed on the three animals used
during analysis development (hereafter \emph{development worms}) and
again on two animals reserved since the study's declaration and opened only
after predictions were fixed---with the strongest anatomy correlations of
the five ($+0.30$, $+0.34$). The top-10 most-driven neurons are 10/10 command/motor class in two of
the three development worms, with the command core topping the two held-out
rankings as well: AVA, AVE, RIM, AIB, RIB, RIV, RME, SMDV and
ventral-cord motor neurons---the canonical motor-command ensemble of the
immobilised worm \citep{kato2015}, obtained without access to anatomical
or functional annotations.

\textbf{Intervention.} The same pipeline on five worms with the
reverse-command hub AVA silenced via a histamine-gated channel
\citep{kato2015}. Directions stated in the working ledger before the
silenced-worm data was opened: the silenced hub shows the largest decrease within the command core (drivenness percentile $0.92\!\to\!0.38$); its
module and motor pool collapse (AVE $-0.28$, RIB $-0.29$, VB01 $-0.64$,
RMED $-0.49$, VA01 $-0.45$); ghost scores remain at zero in all five animals. Two further observations emerge from the pooled per-cell contrast:
\textbf{RIM and AIB retain drive almost unchanged} ($-0.08$, $-0.08$,
class-pooled across the five-animal cohorts, though not uniformly in every
single cell---one silenced animal's RIMR sits low while its RIML tops the
ranking) while every other module member collapses, consistent with the
strong AIB$\to$RIM anatomical route operating independently of AVA
\citep{piggott2011}; and
\textbf{drive reorganises onto the forward hub AVB} ($+0.34$; AVB is
recorded in three of the five silenced animals and the rise is carried by
two of them, so this observation is the weaker of the pair). We report these as
candidate observations pending adjudication against the reversal-circuit
literature \citep{gray2005,sordillo2021}; independent of whether these observations are novel, an observational statistic tracked a targeted genetic intervention
neuron-by-neuron in the stated directions.

\subsection{Zebrafish: a whole-vertebrate-brain map}

ZAPBench provides 71{,}721 segmented neurons $\times$ 7{,}879 timesteps of
light-sheet imaging in a larval zebrafish \citep{zapbench}. On a
2{,}241-step window (open-loop, rotation, dark), the full-brain excess map is computed in minutes on a laptop GPU. The ghost scores $-0.031$; $\sim$2{,}800 neurons clear the $+0.01$
excess threshold; and the driven core is spatially
coherent: the top-1{,}000 neurons are $1.78\times$ more tightly packed than
random draws (median nearest-neighbour distance 16.8 vs.\ $29.8\pm0.6$),
concentrated posteriorly and against the midline---consistent with the
hindbrain/midline territory where internally generated motor dynamics are reported to reside
\citep{dunn2016}---in a window dominated by the dark condition. Region
names await atlas registration and are not claimed
(Figure~\ref{fig:fish}).

\subsection{Clinical EEG: a confirmed effect and an instructive null}

CHB-MIT scalp EEG, subject chb01: 23 channels, 256\,Hz; six of the seven
annotated seizures are analysed (the seventh record was not in our
retrieved subset) \citep{shoeb2009,physionet}, in a field where seizures are increasingly
understood as network-organisation disorders
\citep{kramer2012,friston2011,vicente2011}. One caveat governs all outputs:
seizure-onset zones are \emph{sources}, and sources are invisible to
this statistic by design---the statistic maps the spread network, not the
origin.
Results: max $|$ghost$|$ 0.034 across the 14 windows (no numeric
tolerance was pre-specified; ghost magnitudes across the other deployments
range from $\sim$0.000 on the synthetics to $0.041$ on one wild-type
worm); drivenness \emph{concentration} (top-4 channel share) is
higher during seizures than between them (0.68 vs.\ 0.47; five of six
within-record pairs, the exception---record 03---in the opposite
direction)---seizures recruit the network into a driven regime
(Figure~\ref{fig:eeg}).

\subsection{Climate: a tropical driven core and an invisible solar forcing}

Seventy-seven years of daily sea-level pressure (NCEP/NCAR Reanalysis-1;
10{,}512 cells, $\sim$28{,}000 days) \citep{kalnay1996}, deseasonalised and
differenced, with the SILSO daily sunspot number \citep{silso} embedded as
an additional channel. Pre-registered gates: ghost $-0.005$;
\textbf{sunspot channel $-0.003$}---a real exogenous forcing, embedded
among ten thousand atmospheric variables, scores nothing, the cleanest
field test of the statistic's designed insensitivity to exogenous
sources; and the driven core is spatially
clustered (top-500 median nearest-neighbour distance 0.0435 vs.\
$0.0676\pm0.0048$ under a spatial null). The exploratory map, fixed to
carry no prior claim, reproduces well-established large-scale structure: 99\% of the 500
most-driven cells lie within $30^{\circ}$ of the equator (mean $|$lat$|$
$6.1^{\circ}$ vs.\ $45.6^{\circ}$ for the grid)---the Walker/ENSO belt
\citep{wallace1981,bjerknes1969},
where tropical pressure is subordinate to planetary-scale
circulation \citep{bjerknes1969}---while
polar and storm-track cells, dominated by locally generated baroclinic
dynamics \citep{chang2002}, rank most autonomous. Every cell shows positive excess; a single connected fluid contains no
autonomous members (Figure~\ref{fig:climate}).

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

(1) Drivenness only; sources are invisible, and a source-detection
complement is future work. (2) Top-$k$ semantics; weakly driven members are
indistinguishable from non-members. (3) Validity is established down to $n\approx2{,}000$ samples; no
learned variant we tested succeeded on a 48-point circadian dataset, and
the intermediate regime is unmeasured. (4)
Stationarity is an essential assumption. (5) Single recordings per condition in the biological analyses; the
intervention contrast is five animals per cohort from one preparation; the
EEG analysis covers one subject; absolute excess magnitudes on the worm are small (top-band 0.02--0.23
across cohorts); synthetic validation is confined to map-type generator
families. (6) The self-model's function class governs transfer;
the ghost and known-root channels are the practical guard. (7) The
statistic estimates a known causal quantity; the contribution is scale,
controls, failure analysis and validation, not the concept.

\section{Conclusion}

Three failure mechanisms explain why learned causal readouts break on
redundant dynamical systems; the correction they imply---excess over
self-predictability, computed against a compressed code of everything
else---turns chance-level detection into high-precision membership
scans. It does so at widths where pairwise scanning is infeasible in our
wall-clock measurements and local conditioning is sample-starved, and it
behaves as theory predicts across worm, fish, clinical and geophysical
data, including under a genetic intervention. The operational protocol that found every failure here
(embedded ghosts, known-root channels, trajectory checkpointing,
pre-registration, precision-recall against stated baselines, published
negatives) is, we believe, as transferable as the statistic itself.

\paragraph{Data and code.} All datasets are public and unrestricted (OSF, ZAPBench, PhysioNet, NOAA
PSL, SILSO, NCBI GEO, causal-chamber releases, OpenWorm).
Code, pre-registration documents, and the complete experiment ledger---
including negative results and voided attempts---are released alongside
this paper.

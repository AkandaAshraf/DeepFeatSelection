\title{Excess-over-self predictability: scalable, ghost-controlled detection
of driven variables in large dynamical systems}
\author{Akanda Ashraf}
\date{August 15, 2026 --- preprint draft}

\newtheorem{mechanism}{Mechanism}

\begin{abstract}
Which variables of a large dynamical system are driven by the system, and
which evolve autonomously? Existing causal-detection methods cannot ask this
at scale: we measure full pairwise convergent cross mapping (CCM) at
$\sim$528\,ms per pair ($\approx$14{,}700 compute-hours for one scan of
$10^4$ variables), nearest-neighbour conditioning starved at 18 conditioning dimensions in
our two-seed synthetic tests, and PCMCI with partial correlation acquires
mediated false positives near synchrony on synthetic chains. We first isolate three mechanisms by which
\emph{learned} readouts fail on such systems---maturity collapse of
difference-based importance under Takens redundancy, typicality substitution
in masked-recreation scores, and the inability of ensembling to remove
shared bias---each demonstrated with a separating experimental signature.
These mechanisms jointly imply a corrected statistic---an amortised
Granger/transfer-entropy-type quantity we call
\emph{excess-over-self predictability}: the gain in one-step predictability
of a variable when a learned low-dimensional code of the entire remaining
system is added to a flexible model of the variable's own history. The code
comes from a small ensemble of unsupervised masked autoencoders; each
per-variable readout is a ridge regression, so a complete scan of 71{,}721
variables runs in minutes on a laptop. Every analysis embeds a \emph{ghost
channel}---a circularly shifted copy of a real variable---whose score must
be zero, giving each dataset a built-in falsification test that needs no
ground truth. On synthetic systems with known membership the statistic
attains top-10 precision of 30/30 across three system seeds with zero false
alarms among 907 autonomous channels. Across four open real-world domains
it behaves as theory demands where it should, and fails informatively
where it does: in \emph{C.~elegans} it blindly ranks the
canonical motor-command circuit as the brain's driven core (top-10 all
command/motor in two of three development worms, the command core topping
both held-out rankings; all predictions stated in advance pass in five of
five) and tracks a genetic silencing of the command hub AVA
neuron-by-neuron; in a 71{,}721-neuron zebrafish recording the driven core
is spatially coherent ($1.78\times$ tighter than chance) and
posterior-midline; in clinical EEG (where the statistic maps spread, never onset), seizures
measurably concentrate network drive while per-event spread maps fail to
replicate---itself a useful warning; and in 77 years of daily sea-level pressure the driven core is the
deep tropics (99\% of top cells within $30^{\circ}$ of the equator), while
an embedded solar-forcing channel scores zero within the ghost tolerance
($-0.003$), confirming designed blindness to exogenous roots on real
data. We state semantics and limits
precisely---drivenness only, top-$k$ precision, validated down to
$n\approx2{,}000$ samples---and
release code, pre-registrations, and a ledger including all negative
results.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Causal structure discovery from time series has excellent tools at small
scale. Convergent cross mapping exploits Takens' embedding theorem to detect
coupling between pairs of variables on shared attractors
\citep{sugihara2012,takens1981}; constraint-based frameworks such as PCMCI
condition away spurious associations across moderate variable counts
\citep{runge2019}; and Granger causality and transfer entropy define the
underlying conditional-predictability quantity
\citep{granger1969,schreiber2000}. The question practitioners increasingly
hold, however---\emph{which of my ten thousand variables does this system
drive?}---sits outside every tool's demonstrated envelope. Neural
implementations of Granger causality report tens to $\sim$100 variables
\citep{tank2021}; our own wall-clock measurement of pairwise CCM projects a
single $10^4$-variable scan at roughly 1.7 years of compute; and the
shortcuts one might hope for fail in our tests (cross-mapping against
principal-component summaries scores its own control at the maximum;
$k$NN-based conditioning collapses at 18 conditioning dimensions in a
two-seed synthetic test).

This paper makes three contributions. \textbf{(i)} We isolate, with
separating experimental signatures, three mechanisms by which learned
causal readouts fail on redundant dynamical systems
(\S\ref{sec:mechanisms}). \textbf{(ii)} We derive from them a corrected,
scalable statistic with a built-in falsification control
(\S\ref{sec:method}), and validate it where truth is known
(\S\ref{sec:synthetic}). \textbf{(iii)} We deploy it across four open
real-world domains---including an interventional test in which the
statistic tracks a targeted genetic silencing neuron-by-neuron---and
distil a measured method-selection map (\S\ref{sec:real}--\ref{sec:map}).
Registration discipline varied by study and we state it exactly: the EEG
and climate deployments were registered in a protocol document before their
data was accessed; the wild-type worm predictions were fixed in the analysis
script's header before execution, with two animals reserved untouched for
held-out confirmation; the intervention directions were stated in the
working ledger before the silenced-worm data was opened; the zebrafish
analysis was exploratory apart from its in-code ghost gate. Negatives are
reported at the same volume as positives, and every number below is
reproducible from the released code and public data.

\section{Three failure mechanisms}
\label{sec:mechanisms}

All experiments in this section use coupled logistic-map networks (with
heterogeneous and stochastic variants) where ground truth is known by
construction; full protocols are in the released ledger. Every analysis
embeds a \emph{ghost channel}: a circularly shifted copy of a real
variable, carrying realistic marginals and autocorrelation but no temporal
alignment. Any score the ghost earns is artifact by construction.

\begin{mechanism}[Maturity collapse of difference-based importance]
\label{mech:maturity}
Difference-based scores (leave-one-covariate-out and relatives) measure a
trained model's loss when one input is removed. When a cause's imprint is
redundant across several observed effects---generic for dynamical systems,
since any single effect's delay embedding reconstructs its driver
\citep{takens1981}---the difference collapses to zero \emph{precisely when
training succeeds}: a mature model re-routes around any single removed
input. The separating signature, distinct from overfitting: on a
hub-structured network, checkpointing each model across its own training
trajectory shows redundant-parent scores rising, peaking at partial
maturity, and collapsing to zero by epoch 50, while unique-parent scores
grow monotonically throughout ($0.02\!\to\!0.28$; two system seeds---the
crossing is the synthetic signature). On real neural data
(\emph{C.~elegans}, dense connectome redundancy) the collapse endpoint
replicates: mature leave-one-out gains are exactly zero---AUROC $0.500$ to
three decimals in all three worms tested---and no intermediate checkpoint
opens a usable window. This is the
function-class dependence of importance gaps \citep{xu2020} expressed along
the training trajectory: such scores work \emph{because of} restricted
capacity, not despite it.
\end{mechanism}

\begin{mechanism}[Typicality substitution in masked recreation]
\label{mech:typicality}
Scoring a channel by how well a trained masked autoencoder
\citep{he2022mae} recreates it from the rest is an attractive
foundation-model-style probe of coupling. At scale, on homogeneous
pools, it inverts: on a
1{,}000-channel benchmark (100 coupled members among 900 autonomous
channels), recreation ranks periodic autonomous channels highest (0.76),
the ghost at 0.74, chaotic autonomous channels at 0.29, and true members
last (0.25). The decoder's loss-optimal completion of a masked slot is the
\emph{family-typical} trajectory, so recreation measures typicality, not
coupling---and coupled channels, perturbed off the family manifold by their
drive, are the least typical. Bottleneck width ($8\!\to\!256$), fill values,
and masked-only losses all fail to repair the inversion, because none
changes the optimal blind completion.
\end{mechanism}

\begin{mechanism}[Ensembles average variance, not bias]
\label{mech:ensemble}
The natural rescue---train many models from independent initialisations and
trust the consensus---was tested directly: eight masked autoencoders on one
fixed system, only the seed varying. The consensus average-precision curve
rises with ensemble size ($0.111\!\to\!0.148$) with the expected
$1/\sqrt{M}$ deceleration, exactly as the law of large numbers predicts,
and converges to the wrong place: the ghost's recreation score is
$0.740\pm0.004$ \emph{across the eight independent minima}. The typicality
artifact is a property of data-plus-objective, reproduced in every basin;
averaging over minima cannot touch it.
\end{mechanism}

\section{The excess-over-self statistic}
\label{sec:method}

The three mechanisms point at one repair. Let $x_q(t)$ be variable $q$
after per-variable standardisation and first-differencing (stationarity is a prerequisite; on drifting data the circular-shift
ghost inflates and the scan must be discarded rather than thresholded---the
ghost must be checked, it does not announce failure). Form delay embeddings ($E=3$
throughout) and the joint state $Z(t)$.

\paragraph{Code.} Train $M$ masked autoencoders (independent
initialisations) on $Z$: random channel subsets are corrupted each batch,
the loss is computed on masked positions only \citep{he2022mae}, and a hard
linear bottleneck of width $b \ll \dim Z$ yields codes $c_m(t)$.

\paragraph{Readout.} For each variable $q$ and model $m$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{excess}_m(q) \;=\;
R^2\!\left[\,x_q(t{+}1)\;\middle|\;\phi\big(\text{own lags of } q\big)\oplus
c_m(t)\right]
\;-\;
R^2\!\left[\,x_q(t{+}1)\;\middle|\;\phi\big(\text{own lags of }
q\big)\right],
\label{eq:excess}
\end{equation}
with both terms ridge regressions fit on a training segment and evaluated
on a held-out contiguous tail ($R^2$ clamped at zero), and $\phi$ a
polynomial feature map (degree 3). The reported score is the consensus mean
over models. The subtraction cancels typicality by construction (a channel
predictable from its own structure gains nothing from the code); the
statistic is not a difference across redundant alternatives, so
Mechanism~\ref{mech:maturity} does not apply (the architecture-identical
absolute readout was empirically stable across 5--100 encoder-training
epochs at $V{=}30$; the excess readout reuses those saved encoders); and
its lineage is explicit: a
Granger/transfer-entropy-type quantity \citep{granger1969,schreiber2000}
amortised to large $V$ by unsupervised compression---CCM's
convergence-with-library-size criterion makes the analogous self-control
implicitly \citep{sugihara2012}; Eq.~\ref{eq:excess} makes it explicit and
scalable.

\paragraph{Semantics.} Positive excess means the system's state carries
information about $X$'s next step beyond $X$'s own past: $X$ is
\emph{driven}. A pure source is perfectly predicted by its own history and
is therefore invisible---a designed, testable asymmetry: known-exogenous
variables must score zero (verified below on solar forcing; a genetic
silencing approximates it---the silenced hub falls sharply but not to
zero, retaining residual input).

\paragraph{The self-model's function class is load-bearing.} With a linear
self-baseline the correction fails (a linear fit of a quadratic map leaves
residual self-structure that the code then ``explains'', scoring periodic
channels $+0.26$); with degree-3 features, self-baselines match theory to
three decimals and the confound is cancelled rather than suppressed. On
real data, where no exact function class exists, the ghost and known-root
channels are the practical check.

\paragraph{Cost.} Training is $\sim$57\,s per model at $V=71{,}721$
(and $\sim$80\,s at $V=10{,}512$) on a consumer GPU with a streaming input
pipeline; each readout is a ridge fit ($\sim$7\,ms per variable); saved
encoders make any new readout an evaluation rather than a retrain.

\section{Validation where truth is known}
\label{sec:synthetic}

Systems of $V\in\{30,1000\}$ logistic-map channels, a minority sub-web
coupled (sparse random DAG), the rest autonomous; membership is truth by
construction. At $V{=}1001$ (94 members, baseline precision 0.094):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
system & precision@10 & precision@20 & false alarms (907 non-members)\\
\midrule
seed 0 & 10/10 & 16/20 & 0\\
seed 1 & 10/10 & 20/20 & 0\\
seed 2 & 10/10 & 20/20 & 0\\
heterogeneous pool & 10/10 & 18/20 & 0\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent All autonomous channels---including 61 periodic ones, a
deliberately stochastic AR(1) family in the heterogeneous pool, and the
ghost---pin at zero excess. Two boundaries, measured: the statistic is a
\textbf{top-$k$ detector} (members whose drive share falls below the
readout's noise floor receive slightly negative excess and rank below the
zero-pinned non-members, so full-population AUROC is uninformative even
when top-$k$ precision is perfect---0.28 here by mechanism, not accident;
average precision and top-$k$ against stated baselines are the correct
metrics); the \textbf{encoder is necessary} (the same readout conditioning
on all raw dimensions with tuned regularisation fails, AP 0.10 vs.\ 0.24);
and \textbf{ensembling is helpful but not critical} (single-model AP
0.23--0.28 vs.\ consensus 0.24; its chief value is the cross-model variance
diagnostic). ``False alarm'' throughout means excess above the ghost floor;
non-member excess pins at or below zero to the reported precision.

\section{Real-world validation and deployments}
\label{sec:real}

All datasets are public and unrestricted. Registration status is stated
per study below; the released protocol document and analysis-script headers
are the citable artifacts.

\subsection{\emph{C.~elegans}: the motor-command circuit, found blind---and an interventional test}

\textbf{Wild type.} Whole-brain calcium imaging of five immobilised worms
($\sim$110--135 neurons, $\sim$3{,}100 samples) \citep{kato2015}.
Predictions fixed in the analysis script before execution: ghost
$\approx 0$; sensory neurons (hand-curated canonical classes; $n{=}8/6/1$
per development worm, so per-worm evidence is weak though the direction is
uniform) less driven than command/motor neurons in the constant
environment; positive correlation with anatomical in-weight
\citep{white1986,cook2019}. All three passed on three development worms and
again on two animals reserved since the study's declaration and opened only
after predictions were fixed---with the strongest anatomy correlations of
the five ($+0.30$, $+0.34$). The top-10 most-driven neurons are 10/10 command/motor class in two of
the three development worms, with the command core topping the two held-out
rankings as well: AVA, AVE, RIM, AIB, RIB, RIV, RME, SMDV and
ventral-cord motor neurons---the canonical motor-command ensemble of the
immobilised worm \citep{kato2015}, produced by a statistic with no access
to biology.

\textbf{Intervention.} The same pipeline on five worms with the
reverse-command hub AVA silenced via a histamine-gated channel
\citep{kato2015}. Directions stated in the working ledger before the
silenced-worm data was opened: the silenced hub falls hardest
among the command core (drivenness percentile $0.92\!\to\!0.38$); its
module and motor pool collapse (AVE $-0.28$, RIB $-0.29$, VB01 $-0.64$,
RMED $-0.49$, VA01 $-0.45$); ghosts pin in all five animals. Two further
observations replicate five-of-five: \textbf{RIM and AIB retain drive
almost unchanged} ($-0.08$, $-0.09$) while every other module member
collapses, consistent with the strong AIB$\to$RIM anatomical route
operating independently of AVA; and \textbf{drive reorganises onto the
forward hub AVB} ($+0.34$, excess turning positive). We report these as
candidate observations pending adjudication against the reversal-circuit
literature \citep{gray2005,sordillo2021}; independent of their novelty
grade, an observational statistic tracked a targeted genetic intervention
neuron-by-neuron in the pre-registered directions.

\subsection{Zebrafish: a whole-vertebrate-brain map}

ZAPBench provides 71{,}721 segmented neurons $\times$ 7{,}879 timesteps of
light-sheet imaging in a larval zebrafish \citep{zapbench}. On a
2{,}241-step window (open-loop, rotation, dark), the full-brain excess map
computes in minutes on a laptop GPU. The ghost pins at $-0.031$; $\sim$2{,}800 neurons clear the $+0.01$
excess threshold; and the driven core is spatially
coherent: the top-1{,}000 neurons are $1.78\times$ more tightly packed than
random draws (median nearest-neighbour distance 16.8 vs.\ $29.8\pm0.6$),
concentrated posteriorly and against the midline---consistent with the
hindbrain/midline territory where internally generated motor dynamics are
reported to reside---in a window dominated by the dark condition. Region names await atlas registration and are not claimed.

\subsection{Clinical EEG: a confirmed effect and an instructive null}

CHB-MIT scalp EEG, subject chb01: 23 channels, 256\,Hz; six of the seven
annotated seizures are analysed (the seventh record was not in our
retrieved subset) \citep{shoeb2009,physionet}. One caveat governs all outputs:
seizure-onset zones are \emph{sources}, and sources are invisible to this
statistic by design---the tool maps the spread network, never the origin.
Results: max $|$ghost$|$ 0.034 across the 14 windows, noisier than the
large-$n$ runs (where $|$ghost$| < 0.01$; no numeric tolerance was
pre-specified); drivenness \emph{concentration} (top-4 channel share) is
higher during seizures than between them (0.68 vs.\ 0.47; five of six
within-record pairs, the exception---record 03---in the opposite
direction)---seizures recruit the network into a driven regime. The third pre-registered
prediction failed, and the failure is the useful result: per-event driven
patterns do not replicate across the same patient's seizures (mean pairwise
Spearman 0.08 over 15 pairs). Whatever the cause---genuine per-event
variability or single-window noise---the practical warning stands: single-event connectivity maps at these
window lengths should not be treated as patient signatures without
per-patient replication; we tested one subject.

\subsection{Climate: the tropics are the driven core; solar forcing is invisible}

Seventy-seven years of daily sea-level pressure (NCEP/NCAR Reanalysis-1;
10{,}512 cells, $\sim$28{,}000 days) \citep{kalnay1996}, deseasonalised and
differenced, with the SILSO daily sunspot number \citep{silso} embedded as
an additional channel. Pre-registered gates: ghost $-0.005$;
\textbf{sunspot channel $-0.003$}---a real exogenous forcing, embedded
among ten thousand atmospheric variables, scores nothing, the cleanest
field test of designed root-blindness; and the driven core is spatially
clustered (top-500 median nearest-neighbour distance 0.0435 vs.\
$0.0676\pm0.0048$ under a spatial null). The exploratory map, fixed to
carry no prior claim, reproduces textbook structure: 99\% of the 500
most-driven cells lie within $30^{\circ}$ of the equator (mean $|$lat$|$
$6.1^{\circ}$ vs.\ $45.6^{\circ}$ for the grid)---the Walker/ENSO belt,
where tropical pressure is slaved to planetary-scale circulation---while
polar and storm-track cells rank most autonomous. Every cell shows positive
excess: one connected fluid has no autonomous members.

\section{A measured method-selection map}
\label{sec:map}

Because the goal is a usable tool, we summarise where each method earned
trust in our measurements, including where ours should not be used.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{p{5.2cm}p{4.2cm}p{5.2cm}}
\toprule
regime & recommendation & basis (measured)\\
\midrule
pairs / small systems, weak coupling & CCM & detects coupling 0.01 in
seconds; learned methods need 0.04--0.08\\
short real series ($n \lesssim 50$) & conditional $k$NN first (best AP,
1.75$\times$ baseline); CCM and PCMCI beside it & measured on gene-circuit
data \citep{hughes2009}; no trained estimator viable\\
noise-driven, near-linear systems & PCMCI or CCM & statistically

strong coupling near synchrony & conditional learned models
(\emph{candidate only}: synthetic chains, 3 seeds, unverified; did not

membership at $V \gtrsim 10^3$ & this statistic & pairwise CCM is

\bottomrule

\noindent Absolute expectations matter as much as rankings: on real data
the best methods deliver roughly 1.5--2.4$\times$ baseline average
precision---far from clean recovery.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

(1) Drivenness only; sources are invisible, and a source-detection
complement is future work. (2) Top-$k$ semantics; weakly driven members are
indistinguishable from non-members. (3) validated down to $n\approx2{,}000$ samples; a 48-point circadian
dataset defeats every learned arm we tested, and the intermediate regime is
unmeasured. (4)
Stationarity is load-bearing. (5) Single recordings per condition in the biological analyses; the
intervention contrast is five animals per cohort from one preparation; the
EEG analysis covers one subject; absolute excess magnitudes on the worm are
small (0.02--0.11); synthetic validation is confined to map-type generator
families. (6) The self-model's function class governs transfer;
the ghost and known-root channels are the practical guard. (7) The
statistic estimates a known causal quantity; the contribution is scale,
controls, failure analysis and validation, not the concept.

\section{Conclusion}

Three failure mechanisms explain why learned causal readouts break on
redundant dynamical systems; the correction they imply---excess over
self-predictability, computed against a compressed code of everything
else---turns chance-level detection into high-precision membership scans at
widths where pairwise scanning is measured-infeasible and local
conditioning starves, and behaves as theory demands across
worm, fish, clinical and geophysical data, including under a genetic
intervention. The operational protocol that found every failure here
(embedded ghosts, known-root channels, trajectory checkpointing,
pre-registration, precision-recall against stated baselines, published
negatives) is, we believe, as transferable as the statistic itself.

\paragraph{Data and code.} All datasets are public and unrestricted (OSF, ZAPBench, PhysioNet, NOAA
PSL, SILSO, NCBI GEO, causal-chamber releases, OpenWorm).
Code, pre-registration documents, and the complete experiment ledger---
including negative results and voided attempts---are released alongside
this paper.
